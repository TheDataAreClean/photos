One of my favourite parts of going to Cubbon, the central park of Bengaluru, every weekend is meeting the dogs there. It has now become a ritual that both sides look forward to.

But now since it is summer, they are napping half the time I am there. While I miss hanging out with them, I am glad they are getting a respite from the heat.
